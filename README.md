## What This Program Does:
This program evalulates software license orders and applies standard pricing logic. It calculates pricing discounts based on ordered seat quantities, adds necessary onboarding fees, and flags orders requiring custom contract reviews or dedicated trainers. Additionally, it provides helper functions to summarize order totals across multiple clients.
## Business Rules:
1. License cost is calculated as the price per seat multiplied by the total number of seats.
2. Orders under 10 seats receive no discount; orders between 10 and 49 seats receive a 7% discount; orders with 50 or more seats receive a 16% discount for education customers or a 7% discount for standard customers.
3. The 16% discount is strictly reserved for education customers; non-education orders of 50+ seats receive 7%.
4. A $60 onboarding fee is added to the order total if the discounted cost is $400 or less.
5. Orders with a total cost greater than $5,000 require a formal contract review.
6. Dedicated trainers are provided for orders with 25 or more seats or a total cost exceeding $3,000.
